Reversi

Black 2
White 2

How to play
  • Black moves first. Players take turns placing one disc of their colour.
  • A move must outflank one or more of the opponent's discs: they must lie in a straight line (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) between the new disc and another disc of your colour.
  • All outflanked discs are flipped to your colour.
  • If you have no legal move, your turn is passed. The game ends when neither player can move.
  • The player with the most discs at the end wins.
